About Us

We believe great creative work doesn't happen by accident. It comes from asking the right questions, understanding your audience, and knowing when a printed page will outperform a pixel, or the other way around.

CY Consultants was founded by creative professionals who have spent years working across publishing, broadcast, design, marketing, and digital production. We've seen what works, what doesn't, and what gets lost when strategy and execution don't talk to each other. Our job is to close that gap.

Our Services

01↗

Creative Strategy

Before anything gets made, it needs a purpose. We help you define your message, understand your audience, and build a creative roadmap that ties every piece of content back to your goals.

02◇

Brand Development and Identity

From naming and visual identity to tone of voice and brand guidelines, we help you build a brand that feels consistent and unmistakable across every touchpoint, from business cards to billboards to social feeds.

03▤

Print and Physical Media

Print is far from dead; it's more deliberate than ever. We advise on publications, packaging, signage, editorial design, exhibitions, and printed collateral, including sourcing, production workflows, and finding the right partners.

04↝

Media Transformation

For organizations with traditional media roots, we help plan the shift to digital without losing what made you great: rethinking workflows, teams, tools, and business models for how audiences consume content today.
